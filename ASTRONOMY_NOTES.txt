ASTRONOMY NOTES
===============

The conversion deliberately uses real astronomical names as anchors. The
civilizations and settlement details remain fictional.

Primary anchors:
- Sol / Earth
- Proxima Centauri b
- Ross 128 b
- GJ 273 b
- TRAPPIST-1 e
- TRAPPIST-1 f
- LHS 1140 b
- Teegarden's Star b
- Barnard b

Additional active navigation-system slots are mapped to real stellar systems:
- GJ 667 C
- Epsilon Eridani
- Wolf 1061
- GJ 1061
- HD 40307
- HD 20794

The installer preserves all old .system filenames so that current references
and coordinates keep working; only the in-game system labels are changed.

Reference sources used when preparing the pack:
- NASA Exoplanet Archive
- NASA Science Exoplanet Catalog

The astronomical names are observational/catalogue names. The pack does not
assert that any of these worlds actually possess civilizations, breathable
atmospheres, oceans, forests or the fictional conditions described in-game.
