SOURCE CHANGES MADE BY apply_conversion.py
==========================================

src/const.c
-----------
- Replaces race_table with normalized Local Sphere species statistics.
- Keeps MAX_RACE and numeric race indexes unchanged for compatibility.
- Character creation exposes these eight internal IDs:

  RACE_HUMAN        -> Human
  RACE_WOOKIEE      -> Vordan
  RACE_TWI_LEK      -> Lethari
  RACE_NOGHRI       -> Keshari
  RACE_MON_CALAMARI -> Pelagian
  RACE_TRANDOSHAN   -> Drakken
  RACE_VERPINE      -> Veyran
  RACE_DEFEL        -> Noxian

src/comm.c
----------
- Adds is_local_sphere_playable_race().
- Both race-selection loops in nanny_get_new_race() skip non-playable IDs.
- No save-file race renumbering is performed.
